Ingredients

Pizza Base & Toppings

  • 1 ball pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup cooked and cubed chicken breast
  • 1 cup fresh baby spinach (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese (for topping)

Homemade Alfredo Sauce

  • 1 ½ tablespoons butter
  • 1 clove minced garlic
  • ⅓ cup heavy whipping cream
  • 2 teaspoons cream cheese
  • 2 tablespoons shredded Parmesan cheese
  • Dash of garlic powder, salt, and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Set your oven to 450°F (230°C). If using a pizza stone, place it inside to heat up.
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Stir in the heavy whipping cream and cream cheese until smooth.
  3. Add the 2 tablespoons of Parmesan cheese, a dash of garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Simmer on low for 3 to 5 minutes until the sauce thickens into a glossy spread, then remove from heat.
  4. Stretch or roll out your pizza dough on a floured surface to fit your pizza pan or baking sheet
  5. Spread an even layer of the homemade Alfredo sauce over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust. Top with half of the mozzarella cheese, followed by the cubed chicken and spinach. Finish with the remaining mozzarella and a sprinkle of Parmesan cheese.
  6. Bake in the hot oven for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
  7. Let the pizza rest for 2 to 3 minutes before slicing and serving.
